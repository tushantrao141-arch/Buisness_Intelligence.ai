# Five-Minute Judge Demonstration

## 0:00–0:35 — Problem and promise

“Dashboards show that a KPI moved. SilentSignal shows which movement deserves
attention, what connected activity explains it, how trustworthy the evidence is,
and which action the current user may take.” State immediately that all data is
synthetic and the product does not decide wrongdoing.

## 0:35–1:10 — Governed KPI pulse

Open **KPI Pulse**, Compliance Head, WEST. Show the five KPIs, expected versus
actual, materiality, z-score, impact and priority. Open the contract drawer to
prove the owner, grain, sources, drivers and SLA are configuration-backed.

## 1:10–1:55 — Why it changed

Open **Why it changed** and select Linked-Pattern Exposure. Show the waterfall:
expected → ranked mutually exclusive leaves → observed. Point to the green
reconciliation proof: explained equals movement; unexplained is zero. Mention
that no LLM performs arithmetic.

## 1:55–2:45 — Differentiating connected pattern

Open **SilentSignal Investigation**. Show S1's masked relationship view across
accounts and branches, separate pattern strength and evidence confidence, and
supporting/contradicting/alternative evidence. Open the structured packet and
state that it is the only allowed narrative input.

## 2:45–3:25 — Abstention and security

Switch to the NORTH Investigator. Show S3: near-threshold activity exists, but
stale KYC and missing mappings produce ABSTAIN and request information. Then
open **Governance → Security** and show WEST → NORTH detail denied before packet
construction.

## 3:25–4:05 — Governed action and learning

Return to WEST. Open **Actions**. Show that the Compliance Head gets aggregate
capacity actions while the Investigator gets evidence consolidation. Accept or
reject one action and show the persistent audit record. In Feedback, show
correct/partial/incorrect, corrected driver, action decision and role.

## 4:05–4:40 — Measured differentiation

Open **Governance → Method comparison**. Explain: threshold-only misses S1;
movement/proximity detect it but also raise S3; full SilentSignal retains S1 and
abstains on S3. State the limitation: 100% F1 is on four designed scenarios,
not a production-accuracy claim.

## 4:40–5:00 — Close

“The differentiator is not another alert. It is a traceable path from governed
movement to connected evidence, explicit uncertainty and authorised action.”
End on the guardrail: human decision, synthetic data, no automatic filing or freeze.
